1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how WhiteBoard ("we", "us", or "our", package name: com.aistudio.whiteboard.wbdraw) handles information when you use our mobile application. By downloading, accessing, or using WhiteBoard, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to the practices described in this policy.

We are dedicated to providing a secure, creative, and private digital whiteboard experience for students, teachers, creators, and professionals worldwide.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details (such as your legal name, physical address, email address, or contact number) to use WhiteBoard.

Automatically Collected Technical Information (Via Third-Party SDKs):

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, device model, hardware specifications, Android operating system version, and system language.
  • App Performance and Diagnostics: Crash logs, app startup speed, and diagnostic data collected by Google Play Services to ensure application stability and reliability.
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Information regarding ad impressions, clicks, video completion status for rewarded ads, and fraud prevention signals processed securely by Google Mobile Ads.

3. Local Storage of Your Canvas Creations

All drawings, notes, math formulas, chemical diagrams, sticky notes, shapes, and imported images created on the WhiteBoard canvas are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's private sandboxed SQLite database (Room Database) and local internal storage.

  • Your boards, strokes, and sketches are never transmitted to our servers or third parties.
  • When you choose to export your whiteboard as a PNG image or PDF document, the file is generated locally on your device.
  • If you use the Share button, the exported file is passed directly to the app you select (e.g., Gmail, Google Drive, WhatsApp) using Android's secure FileProvider.

4. Device Permissions Used

WhiteBoard follows the principle of least privilege, requesting only the permissions strictly necessary for its features:

Permission Type Purpose & Necessity
android.permission.INTERNET Normal (Install-Time) Required to communicate with Google Mobile Ads servers to request, load, and display advertisement banners and rewarded video ads.
Android Photo Picker (System API) Zero-Permission Allows you to insert images or photos into your canvas. The app uses Android's privacy-safe Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia), meaning our app has zero broad access to your photo library or device storage. Only the exact media file you select is provided to the app.

6. Rewarded Ads & Personalized Advertising

WhiteBoard provides optional Rewarded Ads. Users may voluntarily elect to watch a short sponsored video ad to earn rewards, such as in-app reward points or unlocking VIP canvas tools (e.g., golden styluses, neon pens, blueprint canvas textures). Participation is completely voluntary.

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising:

You have full control over personalized ads on your Android device. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time:

  1. Open the Settings app on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  3. Tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all your whiteboard contents are stored on your local device:

  • Individual Board Deletion: You can delete any individual board at any time from the app's History dialog. Deleting a board permanently removes it from your device.
  • Complete Data Erasure: To remove all local boards, preferences, and cached assets, you can go to Android Settings → Apps → WhiteBoard → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the WhiteBoard application completely and irreversibly removes all local databases, saved boards, and app cache files.

9. Security of Your Information

We value your trust and implement industry-standard security safeguards. Your canvas files and settings are protected by Android's application sandbox architecture, preventing other apps from accessing your private whiteboard data. Network transmissions initiated by Google Mobile Ads and Play Services use secure, encrypted Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) protocols.
