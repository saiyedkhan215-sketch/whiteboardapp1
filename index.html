3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

WhiteBoard operates on a principle of least-privilege. We only request permissions strictly necessary for app features:

  • INTERNET (Network Access): Required to load advertisements provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and to check network connectivity status. It is not used to upload your whiteboard contents.
  • Storage Access & Android Photo Picker: When you import background images or export drawings as PDFs/PNGs, WhiteBoard uses modern Android system pickers and the Android Storage Access Framework (SAF). The app does not request broad file access or access to your private media library.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support the ongoing development and maintenance of WhiteBoard, the app integrates trusted third-party services that may collect diagnostic or advertising identifiers:

Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

We use Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • IP address and general location (country or city level)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, carrier)
  • Ad interaction metrics and performance data

Google's use of advertising data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more and manage your ad preferences via:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google AdMob Privacy and Data Policies

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID.

5. Data Storage and Security

All drawings, math formulas, science diagrams, layers, and whiteboard projects are stored strictly on your local device using Android's encrypted Room SQLite database and private app storage. Because your canvas data never leaves your device:

  • No user account or password is created or stored.
  • We cannot view, access, or restore your drawings from a remote server.
  • Your whiteboard creations are as secure as your personal physical device.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

You maintain full and complete control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete any individual whiteboard canvas, layer, or item directly from within the app interface.
  • Complete App Data Removal: You can permanently erase all stored whiteboards and application cache at any time by going to: Android Settings > Apps > WhiteBoard > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling WhiteBoard immediately removes all locally cached application databases and project files from your device.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

WhiteBoard is designed as a creative and educational utility suitable for students, teachers, and general users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at saiyedkhan206@gmail.com so we can take appropriate steps.

8. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California, you are entitled to specific privacy rights:

  • Right of Access & Portability: All drawings and data are accessible to you on your device and can be exported as standard PDF, PNG, or JPEG formats.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can delete all your stored boards instantly by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.
  • Right to Object: You can opt out of personalized advertising via your Android device's Google Ads settings.
