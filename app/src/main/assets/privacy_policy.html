1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how WhiteBoard ("we", "us", or "our", package name: com.aistudio.whiteboard.wbdraw) handles information when you use our mobile application. By using WhiteBoard, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect or request any personal data (such as names, emails, addresses, or phone numbers) to use the canvas features.

Technical Data via Google Mobile Ads SDK:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) and coarse IP-derived geographic location.
  • Device hardware details, Android OS version, and diagnostic logs.
  • Ad impressions, click activity, and fraud prevention signals.

3. Local Storage of Canvas Data

All drawings, math formulas, diagrams, sticky notes, shapes, and imported images are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's private sandboxed SQLite database (Room Database) and local internal storage. Your drawings are never uploaded to any remote server.

4. Device Permissions

Permission Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Required to load Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and rewarded ads.
Photo Picker (System API) Zero-permission Android picker to let you insert images into your canvas. The app has no access to other photos in your gallery.

5. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

We integrate Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to serve advertisements. Google's privacy policy can be viewed at: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Rewarded Ads & Opt-Out

Rewarded ads are completely voluntary. To opt out of personalized ads or reset your Advertising ID, go to Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

7. Children's Privacy

WhiteBoard complies with COPPA and Google Play's Families Policy and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete any board at any time from the app's History screen. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data.
